ABOUT US

Spitzfm Radio

Spitzfm Radio is for the unsung heroes of live events — the crew. Whether you’re farming the yam fields at 6am, praying at the altar of fire at 7am, working 9am–5pm, or praising God on Eke Market Day (Sunday), we’ve got the right cultural soundtrack for your generation.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to entertain, connect, and bring agents of change closer to their divine selves. We’re here to build a community for the great leaders and catalysts the world needs today. Whether you’re working, praying, singing, studying, cruising, or doing kareeokee with your remote control in hand, we celebrate the culture, the music, and the togetherness of life.
